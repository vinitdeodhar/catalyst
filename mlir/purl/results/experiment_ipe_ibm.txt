legend (metrics beyond fidelity -- spec 8.2.1):
  lam            global noise scale (0 = noiseless, 1 = device, 2/4 = noisier)
  depth/iter     B = 12 gate-layers per loop body (purl.body_layers)
  *_iters        realized trip count = RUNTIME coherent depth (unbounded arm); max_iters is
                 the decohering TAIL; runtime_depth = mean_iters x B [resource, family 3]
  bounded_cap    C_refresh x B = 24 layers -- the compile-time coherent-depth CAP the cut
                 guarantees, independent of any noise model [resource, family 3]
  fidelity       delivered-state Bloch fidelity F (higher=better), mean +/- seed-std. arms:
                 unbounded (control); refresh(g1) = deterministic gamma=1 cut (proven state,
                 zero variance); knit(g4) = gamma^2=16 quasi cut
  knit(g4)*      *reconstructed (|a|<=1 clip, spec 5.1); n/a where the KNIT variance window is
                 empty -- see V(C_min) in the header [sampling cost, family 2]
  RMSE           delivered-state error sqrt((1-F)^2 + seed-std^2): systematic infidelity (bias)
                 + statistical spread [accuracy, family 1]. Lower better.
  d(ref-unb)     RMSE(refresh) - RMSE(unbounded): <0 => cutting wins (fire refresh); >=0 =>
                 cutting does not pay (decline) [decision quality, family 4]
  best           arm with min measured RMSE (oracle); regret = RMSE(pass-chosen) - RMSE(best)
  header:        V(C_min) = KNIT variance inflation [family 2]; E[#cuts] = expected cuts/shot
                 [family 3]; refresh C-sweep vs window bracketing [family 4, S4]
